Questions

Does JobPicks apply to jobs for me?

No. JobPicks helps you find, evaluate, and prepare applications. You review and submit every application yourself.

Where do job listings come from?

We aggregate listings from legitimate job APIs and employer sources. Listings can be stale or incomplete — always verify on the employer's site.

Is my resume used to train public AI models?

No. Your resume is used to match jobs and power AI features for your account. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Who is JobPicks for?

US-based job seekers who want fewer, better-matched roles — especially in tech and knowledge work — without application spam.
